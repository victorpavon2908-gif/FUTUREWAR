# HELIX BIOFORGE

FUTUREWAR enemy-faction art and gameplay direction.

## Visual language

HELIX units are original biomechanical war organisms: layered dark alloy carapace, exposed synthetic tissue, sharp silhouette breaks, glowing energy organs and asymmetrical integrated weapons. They should read clearly on a mobile screen before fine detail is visible.

Energy colors communicate combat role:

- amber: Glyph frontline units
- violet: Phase mobility units
- cyan: Threshborn heavy units
- red: Carapace beast units

The design intentionally avoids copying any existing franchise model, armor layout or proprietary asset.

## Enemy Pack 01 — implemented

### Glyph Crawler
Frontline gunner. Squat silhouette, armored dorsal mass, orange bio-core and compact rifle. Advances, strafes and retreats to maintain pressure.

### Phase Lancer
Agile skirmisher. Tall narrow body, crest/spines, violet energy lance-rifle and lateral phase-dash behavior.

### Threshborn
Heavy brute. Large hunched silhouette, oversized shoulders, cyan chest core and integrated arm cannon. Can fire at range and perform a close shockwave slam.

### Carapace Crawler
Quadruped assault beast. Low armored body, four limbs, dorsal spines and red sensory nodes. Rushes the player and attacks at melee range.

## Pack 02 — planned

- Blademaster — elite melee warrior with energy blade and dash attacks.
- Bulwark — shield sentinel that creates moving cover for nearby units.
- Void Reaver — berserker with erratic close-range attack chains.
- Helix Archon — commander / boss with summons, area denial and phase abilities.

## Production goal

Procedural geometry is the current in-engine prototype representation. Final production quality should replace these procedural bodies with original rigged 3D meshes, animation sets, authored materials, normal maps, VFX and audio while preserving the implemented silhouette and combat roles.
